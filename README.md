### Hi, I'm Wilbur (Cheng-Yu Lai) 👋

I'm a robotics engineer working on **localization and autonomy for marine robots in GPS-denied environments**. I hold an M.S. in Electrical and Control Engineering from NYCU (Taiwan), where I did my research in the [Assistive Robotics Group](https://github.com/ARG-NCTU) under Prof. Hsueh-Cheng Wang. I now work part-time at JetSea AI on GPS-denied localization for autonomous surface vehicles.

**Research interests:** state estimation & sensor fusion · autonomous navigation & control · reinforcement learning for decision-making · cooperative heterogeneous robots (UAV + USV)
